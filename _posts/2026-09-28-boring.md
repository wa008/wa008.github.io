## Boring

I took a casual life for a long time during the past 2 month. I don't set some goals for myself except the regular work time. I can do whatever I can do. This kind of life lasts for a long time, but I found it's more easy to feel bored in this status.

It's fun to try different things, different hobbies, but it's also easy to stop it if I don't set a goal or enjoy that deeply. I'm big fan of pingpong, I will participate the game every Friday evening. But for others, like piano or tennis, I don't have a regular schedule, it's much hard for me to maintain a regularing practice. AKA, it's too easy to stop. 

I don't know the answer, just write it down to see if I can explore some potential solution for that. 

This makes me miss the time when I focus on building. it's the opposite situation. I have plenty of things to do. I almost don't have any free time. I enjoy that a lot, but it's still hard to maintain that kind of status for a long time. Maybe the solution is to be mixture of the two status. 

Reading is a good way to waste time when I'm bored. Reading is a little bit magical because it can bring both reset and thinking at the same time. More important, it can trigger my thinking motivation with low effort.
