## New Hobbies
I tried a lots of thing in recently two months.

### Piano
An electronic piano is much cheaper, lighter, and takes up less space. However, it takes a lot of time to learn. I've been learning for two months, but I spend less time practicing recently than I did in the beginning. Right now, the hardest part for me is playing a different rhythm with each hand—it's like drawing a circle with one hand while drawing a square with the other. The best thing about the piano is that it can replace short videos sometimes. I'll play for a while instead of watching short videos when I have a little bit of free time and don't want to do anything else.

## Reflections

### Lifelong Hobbies
Table tennis is almost my only hobby that I can insist on since when I was a kid. The reason behind that is I can improve it all the time, there isn't a end for this hobby. The compounding effect is a key concept that can help people achieve success. Piano and tennis are like that on some level. I spent time on them, and I can play better in the future; I can enjoy the techniques that I spent effort learning. It's a little bit utilitarian, but it's a good metric to pick hobby when we don't have good ideas.
